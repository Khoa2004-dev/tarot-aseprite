COMPLETE PIXEL TAROT — 78 CARDS

22 Major Arcana + 56 Minor Arcana.
Minor Arcana contains 14 cards in each suit: Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles.
Each card is an individual 97 x 181 pixel .aseprite file.
Each has three editable raster layers: Frame, Artwork, Number/Rank.
Artwork is flattened within its layer; individual figures and objects are not separated.
Roman numerals mark numbered cards; court cards use PAGE, KNIGHT, QUEEN, KING.

Native-size PNG exports are in each previews folder.
Four suit overview sheets and the Major Arcana overview show enlarged pixel art.
Complete_Deck_Overview.png displays all 78 cards at native resolution.

Visual style follows the approved Fool: gold frames, limited palettes,
crisp pixel clusters, and featureless human faces. Compositions are inspired
by the 1909 Rider–Waite–Smith deck and the supplied original pixel cards.
Classical drapery was added to several Major Arcana figures.

AI-assisted artwork generated with the built-in image generation tool,
then converted to the deck canvas and encoded as layered Aseprite files.
